Use Enterprise mode for workplace learning. It frames work around competencies, training modules, cohorts, and readiness.

## Start with a real work task

Choose one task someone should be able to do, such as handling a customer escalation or diagnosing a common fault.

1. Check that your workspace uses **Enterprise** in [mode settings](../../workspaces/modes/).
2. Select relevant use cases: employee onboarding, compliance and policy, role and skill development, or team coaching.
3. [Create an activity](../../activities/create-activity/) with the role, work context, and target competency.
4. Add trusted materials such as your process guide or product manual.
5. Review the practice, feedback, and completion settings, then [preview and publish](../../activities/preview-and-publish/).
6. [Assign the module](../../activities/assign-activity/) to the intended cohort or team space.

## Review readiness

Look at what the learner actually did, what help they used, and whether their response meets the readiness criteria. Use that evidence to choose coaching or another practice task.

A training result is not by itself a certification or permission to perform a high-stakes job. Keep the responsible person's review in the process.

If you are a learner, follow [Start and continue learning](../../learners/start-and-continue/) for the work shared with you.
