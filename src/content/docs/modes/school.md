Use School mode for classroom teaching and student practice. It uses terms such as curriculum, learning goal, activity, class, and mastery.

## Plan a classroom activity

1. Check **School** in [mode settings](../../workspaces/modes/).
2. Select the use cases that fit: classroom teaching, homework and practice, exam preparation, or language teaching.
3. [Create an activity](../../activities/create-activity/) with a clear learning goal and suitable level.
4. Check that the instructions, materials, and help fit your students.
5. [Preview and publish](../../activities/preview-and-publish/) before you [assign](../../activities/assign-activity/) it to the class space.

For example, ask students to compare two explanations of a science result and show which evidence supports their choice.

## Use the work to plan follow-up

[Review progress](../../activities/review-progress/) alongside the student's answers and the help they used. Decide whether the next step should be more practice, a new question, or a conversation.

If you are a student, open your class space or the assigned link from your teacher and follow [Start and continue learning](../../learners/start-and-continue/).
