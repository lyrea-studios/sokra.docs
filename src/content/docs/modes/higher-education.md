Use Higher Education mode for courses, seminars, and academic work. It uses terms such as syllabus, discipline, learning objective, module, and proficiency.

## Build a module

1. Check **Higher Education** in [mode settings](../../workspaces/modes/).
2. Choose relevant use cases: course and seminar delivery, research methods and lab, or thesis and capstone supervision.
3. [Create an activity](../../activities/create-activity/) with the course level, prior knowledge, and learning objectives.
4. Add the readings, papers, or other source material learners should use.
5. Make the expected evidence clear. For example: “Compare two methods, justify the choice, and state its limits.”
6. [Preview and publish](../../activities/preview-and-publish/), then [assign](../../activities/assign-activity/) it to the course space.

## Review the reasoning

Check the work against the objective and evaluation criteria. Look for how a learner uses evidence, explains assumptions, and handles uncertainty. When citations are required, state that expectation and check the sources.

The mode gives you an academic starting point. It does not replace course requirements or an instructor's judgment.

If you are a learner, follow [Start and continue learning](../../learners/start-and-continue/) for your shared work.
