title: Workspaces and spaces
description: Understand where people, shared work, and activities belong.
---
A **workspace** is the shared home for an organization or your own practice. A **space** is a place within it for a team, cohort, course, class, or study group.

## Choose the right workspace

The word **Workspace** is also the UI label for Sokra's personal mode. Organizations in School, Enterprise, and Higher Education modes still have a workspace. See [Workspace modes](../modes/) for the difference.

Use the workspace menu in the sidebar to switch between workspaces you belong to. Check the name before you create or share content.

Your access can differ between workspaces. Being an admin in one workspace does not give you that role in another.

## Use spaces for shared work

Spaces let an author share activities with a team, cohort, course, class, or study group. A reusable activity can be assigned to more than one space, with its own assignment settings in each.

Give spaces clear names so people can find their group. When sharing an activity, check the chosen space and audience before confirming.

## Check who can do what

Your workspace's mode is separate from your role. Choosing Enterprise does not make everyone a manager, and choosing School does not make everyone a teacher.

The app shows tools based on a person's role and access. Workspace owners and admins have management tools that other members may not see.

If someone is missing a space or action, check their account, workspace membership, and access to that space. Sharing a link alone does not grant access.

## Help someone join

Send an invite using the workspace's people or member controls when you have permission. Ask the recipient to open it with the intended account.
