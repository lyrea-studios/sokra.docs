---
title: Account and workspace settings
description: Find your personal settings, download your data, and manage your workspace.
---
Your personal settings and your workspace settings do different jobs. Open **Settings** from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, and check which group you are changing before you save.

## Your account

Under **Personal** you find your own settings:

- **My account:** the **Profile**, **Security**, and **Data & Privacy** tabs. If you cannot sign in, follow [Sign in to Klarisma](../../start/sign-in/) instead.
- **Appearance & language:** theme (system, light, or dark) and app language.

These settings never change the organization's mode.

## Download your data

1. Open **Settings**, then **My account**.
2. Select the **Data & Privacy** tab.
3. Under **Data export**, select **Download my data**.

You get a ZIP file with a machine-readable file and a readable summary. It contains your own learning data and your own chat messages from all organizations you belong to. In shared chats, it contains only your messages and the replies to them. Nobody else can download your data for you.

The same tab shows the log of who accessed your learning data.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** is on the **My account** page. Klarisma sends you an email with a confirmation link that works for 15 minutes. Deleting removes your account and your access. Your learning data (answers, chats, progress) is not deleted automatically yet; the app shows how to request that.

## Your workspace

Workspace owners and admins see an **Organization** group:

- **General:** workspace details, [organization type (mode)](../modes/), and use cases.
- **Login & Security:** sign-in rules and single sign-on for the organization.
- **AI & Tools:** the AI and tool settings for the workspace.
- **Integrations:** connections to other systems, where offered.

Labels follow the app language. If a control is missing or disabled, check that you are in the intended workspace and have permission to manage it.

## After a change

Save the change and wait for confirmation. If a save fails, follow the error before leaving. When you change the organization type, published activities stay unchanged; only future defaults change.

For a problem you cannot solve, open **Help & Learning** in the sidebar and choose **Questions and feedback**.
