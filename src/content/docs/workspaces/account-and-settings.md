Your account settings and workspace settings do different jobs. Check which one you are changing before you save.

## Your account

Use **Account** settings for the personal account and security controls offered to you. If you cannot sign in, follow [Sign in to Sokra](../../start/sign-in/) instead.

Use **Preferences** for your personal app settings, including the appearance options shown there. These settings do not change the organization's mode.

## Your workspace

Workspace owners and admins can manage the organization settings offered to them:

- **Workspace profile** (or **General**): Workspace details, [organization mode](../modes/), and use cases.
- **Access & Login:** The access and sign-in options configured for the organization.
- **Learning & AI:** The learning and AI policies offered for the workspace.

Labels can differ with the app language. If a control is missing or disabled, check that you are in the intended workspace and have permission to manage it.

## After a change

Save the change and wait for confirmation. If a save fails, follow the error before leaving. When changing mode, existing published activities keep their original behavior; check future activity defaults separately.

For a problem you cannot resolve, use **Feedback & Support** in the app.
