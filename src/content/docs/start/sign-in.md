---
title: Sign in to Klarisma
description: Use your own or your organization's account, accept an invite, and recover access.
---
Use your Klarisma account for your own learning, or an account with access to your organization's workspace.

## Use an invitation

1. Open the invite link your organization or group sent you.
2. If you are not signed in yet, choose **Sign in** or **Create account**. Use the email address the invite was sent to.
3. Select **Accept Invite**, then check the workspace name in the sidebar.

If the invite has expired or no longer works, ask the sender for a new one.

## Sign in again

Open [Klarisma](https://app.klarisma.de). Depending on your organization, the sign-in page offers:

- **Email or username** with a password.
- **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**. Microsoft sign-in works with work and school accounts.
- Your organization's own login. After you enter your email, Klarisma shows a **Sign in with …** button for it if your organization requires it.

Use the same method you used before. Signing in with a different email can open an account without your group work. If the page says "No account yet? Your organization will invite you.", ask your organization for an invite.

## Reset a password

For an email-and-password account, select **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset email.

For a Google, Microsoft, or organization login, use that account's recovery process or ask your organization admin.

## Signed in, but missing your group?

Check your account and selected workspace first. Then ask the person guiding your learning or an admin to check that you belong to the right space.
