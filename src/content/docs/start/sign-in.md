---
title: Sign in to Lyrea
description: Use your own or your organization's account, accept an invite, and recover access.
---
Use your Lyrea account for your own Workspace, or an account with access to your organization's workspace.

## Use an invitation

1. Open the invite your organization or group sent you.
2. Sign in with the account the invite was sent to, or follow the account setup shown.
3. Complete the invite flow, then check the workspace name.

If the invite has expired or no longer works, ask the sender for a new one.

## Sign in again

Open [Lyrea](https://app.sokra.io). Choose the sign-in method offered for your account. Your organization may use an organization login provider or an email and password.

Use the same method you used before. Signing in with a different email can open an account without your group work.

## Reset a password

For an email-and-password account, follow the password reset option on the sign-in page. Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset email.

For an organization-managed login, use your organization's account recovery process or ask your organization admin.

## Signed in, but missing your group?

Check your account and selected workspace first. Then ask the person guiding your learning or admin to check that you belong to the right space.
