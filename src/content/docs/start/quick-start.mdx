Start with the workspace where you want to learn or create. If you are joining an organization, use the account it invited.

## 1. Sign in

Open [Klarisma](https://app.klarisma.de) or your invitation link. Use your usual account or your organization's sign-in method. If you cannot get in, follow [Sign in to Klarisma](../sign-in/).

## 2. Check your workspace

Check the workspace name at the top of the sidebar. If you belong to more than one workspace, open that menu and choose **Organizations** to switch. Each workspace has its own people and content.

## 3. Know your mode

Klarisma has four modes: **School**, **Business**, **Higher Education**, and **Personal**. Personal is for self-directed learning. [Compare the modes](../../workspaces/modes/) to see what each changes.

## 4. Choose your next step

- **Learning on your own:** Start with the [Personal guide](../../modes/personal/).
- **Learning with an organization:** Look under **Next up** on your home page and [start an assigned activity](../../learners/start-and-continue/).
- **Creating for others:** [Create an activity](../../activities/create-activity/), then preview it before sharing.
- **Managing a workspace:** Check [workspaces and spaces](../../workspaces/workspaces-and-spaces/) and [mode settings](../../workspaces/modes/).

## If your home looks empty

Check your account and chosen workspace. For shared work, the person who assigned it or your workspace admin can check access. In a personal workspace, an empty view may simply mean you have not added work yet.
