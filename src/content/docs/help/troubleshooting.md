---
title: Find a fix
description: Solve common sign-in, access, saving, and activity problems.
---
Start with the message shown in the app. It often tells you what to do next.

## I cannot sign in

Use the same email and sign-in method you used before. For an organization login, ask your organization admin. For an email-and-password account, use password reset. See [Sign in to Lyrea](../../start/sign-in/).

## My workspace, space, or activity is missing

Check the account you signed in with and the selected workspace. Ask the person guiding your learning or admin to check your membership and the assignment's audience. An activity may also be outside its availability window.

## My draft will not publish

Check that the latest changes are saved. Read the Studio's readiness messages and complete the required sections. See [Preview and publish](../../activities/preview-and-publish/).

## The words or starting settings look wrong

Check the selected workspace and its [mode](../../workspaces/modes/). Mode changes terms and defaults; your role controls access. A workspace owner or admin can change the mode and use cases in general settings.

## A save or upload has an error

Keep the page open and check your connection. Follow the retry or recovery option shown. If a draft has a conflict, resolve it before leaving or trying to publish. Avoid refreshing while you have unsaved work.

## My learner sees an older version

Check which version the assignment uses. A pinned assignment keeps its chosen version. A run already in progress stays on the version it started with. See [Assign an activity](../../activities/assign-activity/).

## I need more help

Use **Feedback & Support** in the app sidebar. Include:

- What you were trying to do.
- The activity or page name.
- What happened, including the error text.
- What you expected, and roughly when it happened.

If you add a screenshot, crop out other people's work and personal details. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.
