## Choose your setting

- **[School](./modes/school/):** Classroom teaching and student practice.
- **[Enterprise](./modes/enterprise/):** Workplace skills, onboarding, and team training.
- **[Higher Education](./modes/higher-education/):** Courses, seminars, and academic work.
- **[Personal Workspace](./modes/personal/):** Your own goals, practice, and progress.

Your [workspace mode](./workspaces/modes/) shapes terms and starting settings. The same core flow connects goals, activities, evidence, and review.

## Find your place

A **workspace** holds your organization's learning or your own practice. A **space** holds work for a team, cohort, course, class, or study group. An **activity** is a learning experience; your mode may call it a training module or module.

These guides use English labels. Terms can differ by mode and language. The tools you see also depend on your access.
