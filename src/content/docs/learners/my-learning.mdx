**My learning** in the sidebar shows what Klarisma knows about your learning, based on your recorded work. It is not the list of your assigned activities; you find those under **Next up** on your home page and under **Activities**.

## Learning record

**Learning record** shows your evidence per goal. It is a view of your work, not a fixed judgment of your abilities. Select **Details** to see the work behind a summary. **Refresh** updates the page after new work.

<ProductScreenshot src={recordShot} alt="My learning with the Learning record tab: goal counts and summaries of recent work per goal." caption="The learning record counts your goals and summarizes what your latest work shows." />

## Ask for a review

If you think a summary is wrong, select **Ask for a review**. Choose the goal, describe what seems wrong or missing, and select **Send for review**. The original entry stays as it is until someone has checked it.

<ProductScreenshot src={requestShot} alt="Review requests tab with the form Something seems wrong and a sent request that waits for review." caption="A sent request shows as pending until it has been reviewed." />

## Practice

**Practice** offers short, voluntary exercises with a date to come back. They stay separate from your formal learning record and grades.

## Review requests

**Review requests** lists your questions and their answers. Open **Review history** to see what happened to a request.

When you have not done any work yet, the page says so. Start an activity first; see [Start and continue learning](../start-and-continue/).
