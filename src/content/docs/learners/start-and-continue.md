---
title: Start and continue learning
description: Open your assigned activity and return to work already in progress.
---
Use your own account so your work belongs to you.

## Start shared work

1. Sign in and choose your organization workspace.
2. Open your group space, or use the assigned activity link the person guiding your learning shared.
3. Open the activity and read its overview.
4. Select **Start** when you are ready.
5. Read the task and use its answer controls to respond.

Opening an activity overview does not start the lesson on its own.

## Continue later

Return to the same assigned activity with the same account. Use **Continue** when it is offered to return to your work.

Before leaving, wait for any answer submission in progress to finish. If Lyrea shows a save or connection error, follow it before closing the page.

## If you cannot start

Check that you are in the right workspace and space. The activity may not be open yet, may have closed, or may be assigned to a different group. The person guiding your learning can check those settings.

If a link asks you to choose a space, choose your group. Ask the person guiding your learning for the assigned link if your group is missing.

## Learning on your own

In a personal Workspace, start with your own goals and available learning work. You do not need to treat every practice step as a class assignment. See [Personal Workspace](../../modes/personal/) for that flow.
