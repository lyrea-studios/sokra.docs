title: Understand My learning
description: See what Klarisma knows about your learning, practice, and ask for a review.
---
**My learning** in the sidebar shows what Klarisma knows about your learning, based on your recorded work. It is not the list of your assigned activities; you find those under **Next up** on your home page and under **Activities**.

## Learning record

**Learning record** shows your evidence per goal. It is a view of your work, not a fixed judgment of your abilities. You can open the work behind each summary.

If you think a summary is wrong, select **Ask for a review**. A trainer checks it, and the answer appears under **Review requests**.

## Practice

**Practice** offers short, voluntary exercises with a date to come back. They stay separate from your formal learning record and grades.

## Review requests

**Review requests** lists your questions to trainers and their answers.

When you have not done any work yet, the page says so. Start an activity first; see [Start and continue learning](../start-and-continue/).
