Use your own account so your work belongs to you.

## Start shared work

1. Sign in and choose your organization's workspace.
2. Find the activity under **Next up** on your home page, under **Activities** in the sidebar, in your group's space, or through the link the person guiding your learning shared.
3. Open the activity and read its overview.
4. Select **Start now** when you are ready.
5. Read the task and use its answer controls to respond.

Opening an activity overview does not start it on its own. If you already know the topic, the overview may offer **I already know this**.

## How Klarisma uses AI

The first time you open an activity, Klarisma explains how it uses AI. Read it and select **Got it**. You can open it again any time with **How Klarisma uses AI** at the bottom of the activity. See [Get help while learning](../get-help/) for what it says.

## Continue later

Return to the same activity with the same account and select **Continue learning**. **Start again** begins a new run.

Before leaving, wait for any answer submission in progress to finish. If Klarisma shows a save or connection error, follow it before closing the page.

## If you cannot start

Check that you are in the right workspace. The activity may not be open yet, may have closed, or may be assigned to a different group. The person guiding your learning can check those settings.

If a link asks you to choose a space, choose your group. Ask the person guiding your learning for the assigned link if your group is missing.

## Learning on your own

In a personal workspace, start with your own goals and available learning work. You do not need to treat every practice step as a class assignment. See [Personal](../../modes/personal/) for that flow.
