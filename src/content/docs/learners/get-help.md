---
title: Get help while learning
description: Ask a useful question, use the help offered, and know when to ask the person guiding your learning.
---
You do not need to know the answer before you begin. Start by reading the task and trying the part you understand.

## Ask a clear question

When the activity offers a tutor or help control, say which part is hard. For example:

> I can find the two sources, but I do not know how to compare their evidence. Can you help me with the first step?

Give the helper enough context to understand your question. The help available depends on the activity and your organization's settings.

## Use help, then try again

Read the hint or explanation and make your own next attempt. If it still does not make sense, explain what you tried and where you got stuck.

AI can make mistakes. If an answer seems wrong or clashes with the task, check the source material and ask the person guiding your learning.

## Get another person's help

The person guiding your learning can help with unclear tasks, missing activities, access problems, or feedback you do not understand. Include the activity name and describe what you expected to happen.

If you are learning on your own, compare the explanation with a trusted source or ask someone with relevant knowledge. For a problem with the app itself, see [Find a fix](../../help/troubleshooting/).
