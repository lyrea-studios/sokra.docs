---
title: Preview and publish
description: Check the learner experience and publish a ready activity.
---
Start with a saved activity draft that you can edit.

## Preview the learner experience

Open the activity's preview and work through it as a learner. Check:

- The first instruction makes the task clear.
- The content and questions match the learning goals.
- The level, wording, and source material suit your learners.
- Tasks and feedback behave as you expect.

An author preview uses separate preview state. It does not count as a learner's real attempt.

## Publish a ready draft

1. Return to the Studio and address any gaps you found.
2. Wait for your latest changes to be saved.
3. Check any readiness messages and complete the required sections.
4. Select **Publish** when it becomes available.

The published version opens as read-only. Publishing makes a version ready to assign; you still need to choose where to share it.

## Change a published activity

Use **Create editable draft** to make further changes. Review and publish that draft when it is ready.

Existing assignments may stay on their assigned version. Check the assignment's version setting before expecting learners to see a change.
