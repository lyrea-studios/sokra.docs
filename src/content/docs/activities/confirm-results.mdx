Klarisma grades each submitted answer first. These grades are provisional. A trainer confirms or changes them and decides the final result, unless the activity lets Klarisma decide (see [Set feedback and completion](../feedback-and-completion/)).

You need to be an admin of the space the activity is assigned to, or an owner or admin of the workspace.

## Find work that needs you

- **Owners and admins:** Open **Review** in the sidebar. Under **Needs attention**, select **Review submitted work**.
- **Space admins:** Open your space and select the **Review** tab, or open the activity and select **Review**.
- **On the home page:** Klarisma tells you when work is ready for review.

The activity review lists each learner with a status such as **Needs review** and an evidence status such as **Provisional assessment**. Select **Review work** for the learner you want to check.

## Read the AI suggestion and the evidence

At the top, **AI suggestion** shows each goal with a provisional status, for example **Open** or **Contradictory**, and how many answers speak for or against it. Below, **Evidence** lists what each answer showed.

## Confirm or change the grade of each part

Under **Grades of the parts**, every answer has a grade: **Correct**, **Partly correct**, or **Not yet correct**. A label shows where the grade comes from:

- **Answer key · fixed:** the answer was checked against a fixed solution.
- **AI · provisional:** Klarisma graded the answer, for example a written explanation.

Select **Confirm** when the grade fits the work. To change it, choose another grade and select **Save**. The answer then shows **Trainer · confirmed**, and the original AI grade stays visible next to it.

## Decide the result

Under **Your decision**, choose **Complete** when the learner has shown the goals, or **Another attempt** when more work is needed. Klarisma marks its own suggestion with **AI suggestion**. Add a **Note to the learner (optional)** that helps with the next step, then select **Submit feedback**.

<ProductScreenshot src={decisionShot} alt="Review page with the AI suggestion per goal, Another attempt selected, and a note to the learner." caption="The trainer asks for another attempt and adds a note. Submit feedback sends the decision." />

The learner sees your decision and your note on the activity page. With **Another attempt**, the learner starts again and repeats the part that is still missing. If the activity holds feedback until a trainer has reviewed it, the learner gets feedback only after you submit.

## Questions from learners

Learners can ask for a review of a goal in **My learning**. Owners and admins answer these requests.

Every time you open a learner's data, Klarisma logs it. The learner can see who opened it and when.
