Start with a saved activity draft that you can edit.

## Preview the learner experience

In the Studio, open **Review & publish** and select **Preview as learner**. Work through the activity as a learner and check:

- The first instruction makes the task clear.
- The content and questions match the learning goals.
- The level, wording, and source material suit your learners.
- Tasks and feedback behave as you expect.

The preview runs in an isolated session. It does not count as a learner's attempt and does not appear in learner results. If you change the draft while a preview is open, Klarisma tells you that the draft changed since the preview started.

## Publish a ready draft

1. Address any gaps you found in the preview.
2. Wait until the Studio shows that your changes are saved.
3. Check the readiness messages under **Review & publish** and complete the missing parts.
4. Select **Publish**.

If problems are still open, Klarisma asks whether to publish anyway. Fix them first unless you are sure they do not matter. Publishing makes a version ready to assign; you still need to choose where to share it.

## Change a published activity

Open the activity in the Studio and edit it. Your first save starts a new draft. Learners keep using the published version until you publish again. The Studio header shows whether you are looking at a draft or the published version, and its version number.

Existing assignments may stay on their assigned version. Check the assignment's version policy before expecting learners to see a change.
