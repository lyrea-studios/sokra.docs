---
title: Review and confirm learner work
description: Check submitted work, confirm or correct the AI assessment, and decide the result.
---
Klarisma gives each submitted answer a first assessment. That assessment is provisional. A trainer confirms or corrects it and decides the final result, unless the activity lets Klarisma decide (see [Set feedback and completion](../feedback-and-completion/)).

You need to manage the space the activity is assigned to, or be a workspace owner or admin.

## Find work that needs you

- **Owners and admins:** Open **Review** in the sidebar. It lists submitted work that waits for a trainer's decision; select **Review submitted work**.
- **Space managers:** Open your space and select the **Review** tab. **Team review** shows who needs a next step.
- **On the home page:** Klarisma shows a card when submissions are waiting.

Open the activity's review. The list shows each learner with a status such as **Needs review** and an evidence status such as **Provisional assessment**. Select **Review work** for the learner you want to check.

## Confirm or correct each goal

Read the learner's answers and the evidence for each goal. Then choose:

- **Approve** when the assessment fits the work.
- **Too harsh** or **Too generous** when it misjudges the work.
- **Reword** when the result is right but the wording for the learner should change.
- **Advanced edit** for a detailed change.

## Decide the result

Choose **Complete** when the learner has shown the goals, or **Another attempt** when more work is needed. Add a **Note to the learner (optional)** if it helps them with the next step.

If the activity holds feedback until a trainer has looked at it, the learner sees the feedback only after you approve it.

## Questions from learners

Learners can ask for a review of a goal in **My learning**. You find these questions on the learning reviews page under **My learning**.

Every time you open a learner's data, Klarisma logs it. The learner can see who opened it and when.
