---
title: Set feedback and completion
description: Choose when learners get feedback, who decides the final result, and whether they may try again.
---
In the Activity Studio, the step for goals and completion decides how an activity ends. Check these three settings before you publish.

## When feedback appears

- **During the Activity:** learners get feedback while they work.
- **At the end:** learners get feedback when they finish.
- **Author reviews first:** feedback waits until a trainer has looked at it and approved it. See [Review and confirm learner work](../confirm-results/).
- **Custom:** your own combination.

## Who decides the final result

- **Klarisma decides from evidence:** Klarisma uses the published evidence rules. A submitted answer alone does not count as mastery.
- **Klarisma recommends, trainer confirms** (Business mode): Klarisma proposes a result, and a trainer confirms it.
- **Trainer decides:** a trainer decides the result.

Labels follow your [mode](../../workspaces/modes/). For workplace training, a trainer should confirm results that matter, such as readiness for a task.

## New attempts

- **Always allowed:** learners can start a new attempt at any time, even after review.
- **Until review:** learners can try again until their work is reviewed. After that, only when the review recommends another attempt.
- **No retries:** learners can complete the activity only once.

## Check before publishing

Under **Review & publish**, use the learner preview to check how feedback appears. These settings belong to the published version. To change them later, edit the activity and publish again; see [Preview and publish](../preview-and-publish/).
