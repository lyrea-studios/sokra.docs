In the Activity Studio, the step for goals and completion decides how an activity ends. In Business mode it is called **Competencies & completion**. Check these settings before you publish.

## Choose how learners finish

Under **When are learners done?**, pick the option that fits the activity:

- **Free practice:** learners submit when they are done. Feedback while they work, unlimited attempts.
- **Self-check:** learners submit and get feedback at the end. Unlimited attempts.
- **Evidence checked by Klarisma:** required goals need enough evidence. Klarisma decides and gives feedback at the end.
- **Evidence with sign-off:** required goals need enough evidence. A trainer reviews first and decides. See [Review and confirm learner work](../confirm-results/).

For workplace training, use **Evidence with sign-off** when a result matters, such as readiness for a task.

## Adjust the details

Select **Adjust completion settings** to change single settings:

- **When learners can finish:** when the learner submits, or when there is enough evidence for the required competencies.
- **Who decides the final result:** **Klarisma decides from evidence** or **Trainer decides**. Klarisma uses the published evidence rules; a submitted answer alone does not count as mastery.
- **When feedback appears:** **During the activity**, **At the end of the activity**, or **Trainer reviews first**. With the last option, learners get final feedback only after a trainer has reviewed their work.
- **New attempts:** **Always allowed**, **Until review** (after the review only when it recommends another attempt), or **No retries**.

Labels follow your workspace [mode](../../workspaces/modes/).

## Check before publishing

Under **Review & publish**, use **Preview as learner** to check how feedback appears. These settings belong to the published version. To change them later, edit the activity and publish again; see [Preview and publish](../preview-and-publish/).
